WHERE CLINICAL MASTERMIND MEETS TIMELESS EMPATHY

Founded in Zurich in 1996, Orynth Medical Sanctuary was conceived by surgeons who rejected industrialized hospital bureaucracy in pursuit of unhurried, physician-owned medicine.

1996 FOUNDED IN ZURICH, SWITZERLAND
THE FOUNDING CONVICTION

RESTORING DIGNITY TO THE PATIENT-PHYSICIAN BOND

“The finest surgical instrument in the world will never replace the profound reassurance of a master physician who sits by your side, listens to your heartbeat, and knows your name.”

Over three decades ago, Dr. Julian Vance and a founding circle of thoracic, neurosurgical, and orthopedic chairs united to construct a sanctuary where patient outcomes—not quarterly balance sheets—governed every protocol.

Today, Orynth operates state-of-the-art medical pavilions across Switzerland, London, and the Mediterranean, maintaining an unwavering 1:1 nurse-to-patient ratio and the lowest surgical complication rate in Europe.
