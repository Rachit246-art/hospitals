CAMPUS ACCESSIBILITY

How to Reach Hero Hospital

Centrally located in the heart of the national capital region with seamless arterial road connectivity, dedicated rapid metro links, and emergency helipad access.

🚇

By Metro Transit

Located just 450 meters from Sector 18 Metro Station (Blue Line). Complimentary hospital shuttle e-rickshaws connect Gate 2 of the metro station continuously every 10 minutes.

🚗

By Car & Complimentary Valet

Directly accessible via Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and DND Flyway. Enter via Gate 1 for 24/7 complimentary valet parking across 500+ secure subterranean bays.

🚌

By Public Bus Routes

The "Hero Hospital Central Terminal" stop is situated directly in front of the main OPD tower, served by DTC routes 34, 49, 102, and intercity feeder buses.

✈️

From Airport & Railway Stations

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) is a 45-minute drive via the DND expressway. New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) is 30 minutes away. International patient pickups are arranged on request.

PATIENT GUIDELINES

Visitor & Admission Guidelines

Help us maintain a peaceful, sterile, and safe recovery environment for all patients.

Please bring government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID), your doctor's admission advice slip, recent diagnostic investigation reports, and your health insurance/TPA card with policy documents for cashless pre-approval.

For Private Deluxe and Presidential Suites, one primary attendant is accommodated overnight with dedicated bedding and dining. In Intensive Care Units (ICU), attendants stay in our designated Attendant Lounge with real-time audio-visual patient status telemetry.

To protect both children and inpatients from nosocomial infections, children under 12 years are restricted from entering ICU and post-surgical recovery wards, except under special clinical permissions.
