HUMBLE BEGINNINGS TO GLOBAL STANDARD

Where Clinical Brilliance
Meets Deep Empathy

"A hospital is not merely brick, steel, and monitors. It is a sanctuary where human vulnerability is met with supreme clinical mastery and heartfelt warmth."

Established in 1994 as a modest 50-bed critical care nursing institute, Hero Hospital was envisioned by pioneer clinicians who witnessed the pressing need for integrated, ethical, and super-specialized healthcare in North India.

Over three transformative decades, we expanded into a 500-bed state-of-the-art medical institute featuring Asia's most sophisticated robotic surgical systems, comprehensive cancer therapy centers, 24/7 cardiac emergency catheterization suites, and premier organ transplant capabilities.

OUR JOURNEY OVER TIME

Milestones in Healthcare Leadership

Explore key milestones that shaped Hero Hospital into an international healthcare institution.

1994

Foundation & First Intensive Care Unit

Opened our doors with 50 emergency beds and the city's first dedicated trauma resuscitation bay, serving over 10,000 patients in the opening year.

2005

Cardiac Catheterization & Heart Tower

Commissioned North India's first fully digital Bi-Plane cardiac cath lab, pioneering primary angioplasty with door-to-balloon time under 45 minutes.

2014

JCI Accreditation & Organ Transplant Center

Awarded prestigious Joint Commission International (JCI, USA) Gold Seal and launched our comprehensive Liver and Renal transplant program with 98% graft survival.

2021

Robotic Surgery Institute & Cancer Wing

Inaugurated dedicated robotic oncology and joint replacement suites, completing over 2,500 minimally invasive procedures with minimal blood loss and rapid recovery.

2026

500-Bed Ultra-Luxury Smart Tower

Unveiled the new smart hospital wing featuring AI-driven patient monitoring, 3T silent MRI, boutique healing suites, and zero-carbon green architecture.
