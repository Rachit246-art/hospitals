OUR FOUNDATIONAL VALUES

The Four Pillars that Guide Every Pulse and Procedure

Patient-First Ethics

No redundant tests, zero unnecessary surgeries, and transparent clinical counseling for families at every turn of diagnosis.

Robotic Precision

Integrating 4th Gen Da Vinci Xi surgical robotics and AI-augmented radiology to turn invasive procedures into pinhole recoveries.

Infection-Free Safety

Adhering to strict positive-pressure laminar airflow OTs, automated UV-C disinfection, and zero-tolerance sepsis protocols.

Rapid Golden Hour

Guaranteed under-4-minute emergency response time with dedicated 24/7 Level-1 trauma, stroke, and STEMI cardiac alert teams.

OUR 28-YEAR HERITAGE

From a 50-Bed Clinic to a National Benchmark of Excellence

1998

Foundation of Hope

Inaugurated as a specialized trauma and heart center with 50 beds, bringing modern critical care to the region for the first time.

2006

Pediatric Cardiac Wing

Established Northern India's first dedicated pediatric cardiovascular surgery unit, providing over 1,200 free surgeries for underprivileged children.

2014

JCI Gold Seal Certification

Awarded prestigious Joint Commission International (JCI) accreditation, validating global standards of patient safety and clinical clinical outcomes.

2019

Robotic Surgery & Organ Transplant

Commissioned the Da Vinci Xi 4th Gen robotic theatre suite alongside a state-of-the-art multi-organ transplant institute (Liver, Kidney, Cornea).

2023

AI Diagnostic Hub & Green Campus

Integrated real-time AI radiology assistance with 3.0T silent MRI, earning LEED Platinum Green Hospital recognition.

2026 & Beyond

Quaternary Smart Healthcare

Expanding to 750 smart hospital suites with automated medicine delivery, telehealth second opinions, and genomic precision medicine.
