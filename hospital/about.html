OUR JOURNEY

28 Years of clinical milestones

Tracking our evolution from a neighborhood diagnostic clinic to a nationally recognized tertiary care hospital.

1998

Foundation of Curevo Clinic

Started as a dedicated outpatient diagnostic center with 12 physicians, focused on preventive cardiology and primary family medicine.

2008

JCI Accreditation & 150-Bed Expansion

Achieved international Joint Commission accreditation and opened dedicated intensive care, surgical wings, and women's health suites.

2016

Center for Robotic Surgery

Inaugurated minimal-access da Vinci surgical suites for cardiology, urology, and gynecological interventions, minimizing recovery times by 60%.

2024 - PRESENT

AI Diagnostics & Digital Campus

Integrated high-speed digital radiology, real-time telemetry patient monitoring, and smart clinical consultation portals for 24/7 care.
